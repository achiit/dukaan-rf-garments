About Us

Ten years of knowing what our customers like

I opened RF Garments in Kapashera in 2016 with a small stock and a big belief: people here deserve good clothes at fair prices, without having to travel across the city.

Over ten years I have learned which fabrics hold up in Delhi summers, which embroidery lasts after washing, and what a family needs when a wedding or festival is a week away. I choose every piece myself.

Come in, touch the cloth, and take your time. If something is not right for you, I will say so.
